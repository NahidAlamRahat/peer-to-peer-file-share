1. Information Collection and Use

For a better experience while using our Service, we may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information. PeerTransfer is a peer-to-peer file sharing application, and we prioritize your privacy. Here is how we handle specific permissions and data:

  • Camera: The app requires camera permission solely for the purpose of scanning QR codes to quickly connect devices. We do not take photos, record videos, or upload any camera feed to any server. All QR code processing happens locally on your device.
  • Files and Storage: The app requires storage permissions to allow you to select files for sharing and to save files you receive. Your files are transferred directly between peers (End-to-End Encrypted via WebRTC). We do NOT store, upload, or access your files on any developer server.

We want to inform you that whenever you use our Service, in a case of an error in the app we collect data and information (through third-party products) on your phone called Log Data. 4. Security

We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. PeerTransfer uses WebRTC DTLS-SRTP for end-to-end encryption during file transfers, ensuring your data cannot be intercepted by us or any middleman.
