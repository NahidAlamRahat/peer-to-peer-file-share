1. Information Collection and Use

For a better experience while using our Service, we may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information. PeerTransfer is a peer-to-peer file sharing application, and we prioritize your privacy. Here is how we handle specific permissions and data:

  • Camera: The app requires camera permission solely for the purpose of scanning QR codes to quickly connect devices. We do not take photos, record videos, or upload any camera feed to any server. All QR code processing happens locally on your device.
  • Storage and Media Files: The app requires read and write access to your device storage (including granular media permissions like Images, Videos, and Audio on Android 13+). This allows you to select media and documents for sharing, and to save received files. Your files are transferred directly between peers (End-to-End Encrypted via WebRTC). We do NOT store, upload, or access your files on any developer server.
  • All Files Access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): On Android 11 and above, the app requires "All Files Access" to enable you to browse, send, and receive files of any format (not just media) across your entire device storage, which is the core functionality of a file-sharing app. We do not use this access for any purpose other than facilitating your peer-to-peer file transfers.
  • Foreground Service (Data Sync): We utilize Android foreground services to maintain uninterrupted file transfers when the app is running in the background. This is strictly used for peer-to-peer data synchronization between connected devices. This background service does not collect or upload any of your personal data or files to our servers.

3. Log Data

We want to inform you that whenever you use our Service, in a case of an error in the app we collect data and information (through third-party products) on your phone called Log Data. This Log Data may include information such as your device Internet Protocol ("IP") address, device name, operating system version, the configuration of the app when utilizing our Service, the time and date of your use of the Service, and other statistics.

4. Security

We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. PeerTransfer uses WebRTC DTLS-SRTP for end-to-end encryption during file transfers, ensuring your data cannot be intercepted by us or any middleman. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.
